A Cinephile's Journal

Film is the
art of our time.

"So cinema, it takes me around the world and it helps me to appreciate and respect differences."

— Tom Cruise

There are countless films that have shaped who I am today, the most notable being Radio, Good Will Hunting, and Sentimental Value. Radio taught me the importance of treating everyone with love and respect. Good Will Hunting taught me to "see about a girl" and taught me the importance of mental health and most importantly the importance of a partner in life. Sentimental Value taught me the importance of family connection.
